Privacy Policy

Paranormal Party · Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Paranormal Party, developed by Echo Willow RBS, handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Overview

Paranormal Party is an offline application featuring interactive tools for paranormal exploration, device-sensor observations, and information about locations, history, and legends. Descriptions of paranormal concepts do not represent a claim that any particular phenomenon has been scientifically established or disproven.

2. Information We Collect

Echo Willow RBS does not operate an account system or application server for Paranormal Party. The application does not request your name, email address, phone number, or precise location as part of its normal operation.

The application uses device sensors, including magnetic-field and motion sensors, to provide its features. Sensor readings are processed on your device. The application does not use the microphone or record audio.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Paranormal Party may store app preferences and feature-related information locally on your device, such as language selection, settings, and locally maintained session or activity history. This information is used to support app functionality and preserve your choices.

Echo Willow RBS does not receive this locally stored information through an application-operated server.

4. Advertising, Analytics, and Sharing

Paranormal Party does not integrate advertising networks, third-party analytics services, or tracking SDKs. Echo Willow RBS does not sell personal information or share app data with advertisers through the application.

5. Android Backup and Device Services

Depending on your device, Android version, account configuration, and backup settings, Android may back up or transfer certain locally stored application data through operating-system services. Such backups or transfers are controlled by Android and the relevant service provider, not by an Echo Willow RBS application server. You can review backup options in your device settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app information may remain on your device while Paranormal Party is installed. You can generally remove the application's local data through Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the app. Copies retained in Android backups may be governed by your device's and backup provider's settings and retention practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Paranormal Party does not knowingly collect children's personal information through an application-operated server. If you have a privacy concern involving a child, please contact us using the address below.

8. Security

The application is designed to operate offline and does not require an account. Device security and any operating-system backup services are subject to the protections and settings provided by your device and service providers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the application or relevant practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

For questions or requests about this Privacy Policy or Paranormal Party, contact Echo Willow RBS at echowillow.support@gmail.com.
